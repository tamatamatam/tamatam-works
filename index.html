Terina is the head: advertising and culture, founder of Priestor festival. Matej is the heart: design, photography and style.

Priestor festival

· · ·

Priestor is ours in the most personal way. Terina founded the festival in Trenčín in 2018 and we have been dressing it up ever since.

Every edition gets a new poster, a new spatial game with the name. The merch carries the same letters, stretched across the back. Seven years in, it still looks like itself and never the same twice.

02

Gastro+

· · ·

Gastro+ feeds people at festivals and in its canteen. We look after everything you see around the food: photos, social media, napkins, textiles and aprons.

Our favourite bit is the tote bag. Hand-drawn letters, a heart and a bit of local cheek, in black and natural.

03

Klinika Vlna

· ·

A dentist is rarely anyone’s favourite place. With Klinika Vlna we tried to change the mood: calm green, a soft wave and a voice that talks about teeth the way people really do.

We built the identity from scratch and keep it alive in social posts, carousels and appointment cards.

04

PARA

Hoodies and sweatshirts for the band PARA, in three colours.

The fun part is the one you don’t see: we scaled the print for every size from XS to XXL, so it sits right on every body.

05

HEX

HEX, one of the best-known Slovak bands, turned 35. We designed the anniversary shirts.

From a drawn band portrait to a back print that tells the whole story of 1990, the year of their first gig. The crew got their own line, HEXPERT. Move over the text shirt to read it.

06

BikeUP

BikeUP rents bikes to kids, who grow out of them every year. Parents liked the idea, but about 70% of them gave up between the cart and the checkout.

We redesigned the whole way from product to payment, so the price is clear from the start. More than 20 screens for mobile and desktop.

Live site: bikeup.sk →

07

Hviezda

· ·

Hviezda is a cultural and creative centre in Trenčín. Its identity came out of a public design competition, and we won it.

Logo, colours, type, a grid for posters and screens, wayfinding and merch. All of it in a 69-page manual, so the system can live on without us.

08

tuduu

· ·

tuduu is our own baby. Terina and Matej started it from scratch: paper planners and notepads for the week, the shopping, the packing and everything in between.

Name, brand, product, photos and posts. All ours, all a little hand-drawn.

09

Čierne diery

Trenčín is full of art hiding in plain sight: mosaics, reliefs, sculptures. Matej photographed them for a book that is now sold out.

He worked closely with the Hlava5 team, who stand behind the mapping project, and with Čierne diery, the publisher of the book.
